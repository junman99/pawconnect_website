If you found a bug, broken link, loading issue, moderation issue, or suspicious behavior in PawConnect, use the details below so we can investigate it properly.

Best contact for product issues

Email: support@pawconnectsocial.com

Send Bug Report

Best contact for privacy or safety concerns

Privacy: privacy@pawconnectsocial.com

Legal / terms: legal@pawconnectsocial.com

What to include

  • What you were trying to do
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • Your PawConnect app version
  • Whether the issue happens every time or only sometimes
  • Any screenshots or screen recordings that help explain it

Before reporting, try these quick checks

  • Force close PawConnect and reopen it
  • Make sure you are on the latest app build
  • Check that your internet connection is stable
  • Sign out and sign back in if the issue looks account-specific

Abuse, impersonation, or harmful content

If the issue is user safety related, please include the username, pet profile, post, or message involved so the report can be reviewed faster.
