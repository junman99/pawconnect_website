About PawConnect

PawConnect is an iOS app built by a pet owner in Singapore, frustrated by the lack of a good local pet community platform. We believe every pet deserves to be celebrated — and every owner deserves a community that understands them.

The app is currently in development. Join the waitlist to get notified when we launch.
