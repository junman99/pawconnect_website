Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open PawConnect and choose Apple, Google, or email sign-up from the welcome screen.

How do I add or edit my pet profile?

Go to your profile tab, open your pet card, and use the edit controls to update your pet's profile, photos, and wellness details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, email privacy@pawconnectsocial.com from the email address tied to your account.

Why do I need location permission?

PawConnect uses location only for features that need it, such as walk tracking and nearby services like vets or parks. You can revoke location access anytime in iOS Settings.

How do notifications work?

You can manage likes, comments, follows, messages, reactions, invites, and reminders in Settings → Notifications.

How do I report a bug or abusive content?

Use the in-app Report a Problem link or open Report a Problem. For urgent safety or privacy issues, contact privacy@pawconnectsocial.com.
