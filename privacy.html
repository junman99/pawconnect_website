1. Introduction

Welcome to PawConnect ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what happens to it when you delete your account.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and a password, or your Apple or Google sign-in identifier, when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile photo, and pet profiles you create
  • Content: Photos, videos, captions, stories, comments, and the paws and reactions you give. A paw is a kind of reaction; we store one paw or reaction per person for each post, with your account, and anyone who can see a post can see who pawed or reacted to it
  • Pet Data: Pet name, species, breed, birthday (if you only know your pet's approximate age, it is stored as an estimated birthday and shown as "estimated"), weight, the microchip number you enter, and the health records, medical documents (including any insurance paperwork you choose to upload), reminders, activity logs (including walks, see Section 2.2), and notes you enter. A pet's profile has a public part that other PawConnect users can see (name, photo, species, breed, gender, bio, and age shown by month) and a private part. The microchip number is kept in a private part that only you and the pet's co-owners can read; the exact birthday, weight, and neutered status are kept in a private part that only you, the pet's co-owners, and the people you have given access to that pet can read. The microchip number appears on the public recovery page only if you choose to show it. Older versions of the app also kept the microchip number, exact birthday, and weight in the public part of the profile, where any signed-in user's app could technically read them; we remove those copies in a one-off clean-up once most people have updated, so until then some older pets may still have them there
  • Emergency Contacts: The names, phone numbers, and types of the vets, sitters, and family members you save for a pet. Please only enter details of people who are happy for you to do so
  • Messages: Direct and group messages you send, including photos, voice notes, and shared posts (see Section 6)
  • Support Conversations: Messages and photos you send to PawConnect Support (see Section 6)
  • Reports and Blocks: Reports you file about content or people, and the people you block (see Section 7)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: Approximate or precise location when you use map-based features (such as Find a Vet) or track a walk, only when you grant permission. Place searches (such as Find a Vet) send your position and what you search for to Google Maps and Places, or to Apple Maps if Google is not available. To work out which country you are in, the app uses the country of your App Store account, then your phone's region setting, and, only if you have already allowed location access, your approximate location (worked out on your device). For every signed-in account we store the country, not the location, to show the right PawShop region and to see where people want PawShop to open
  • Device and Notification Information: Device type, iOS version, app version, the push notification token for your device, and the language you chose in the app and your phone's language (kept with the push token, so notifications arrive in the right language), and a device attestation token (from Apple App Attest and Firebase App Check) that shows requests come from the genuine app. Before you sign in, the app also reads a public file that says which app versions are still supported; it contains no personal data, and, like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the server that provides it
  • Usage and Diagnostics: We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to find and fix crashes. These collect app events (for example sign-ups, adding a pet, creating a post, using reminders, viewing profiles, sharing, and viewing or tapping items in PawShop, including the words you type into the PawShop search box), crash reports, device details, an app-instance identifier, and three settings about you: the number of pets you have, your main pet species, and your region. They are always on in the current version of the app: there is no switch to turn them off
  • Recent Sign-ins: When you sign in, the app records the device name and model, the iOS version, and the sign-in method in a list called Recent Sign-ins, so you can see where your account was used. Entries older than 90 days are deleted
  • Walk Tracking: When you track a walk, PawConnect uses your precise location while the walk is running (and in the background if you allowed "Always"). When you finish the walk, its route, distance, duration, and the estimated calories burned are saved to your pet's activity log on our servers. Everyone who has access to that pet (the owner, co-owners, caregivers, and viewers you invite) can see the walk and its route. You can delete a walk from the activity log at any time. Photos you add to a walk are stored like your other photos

2.3 Information NOT Collected

  • We do NOT access your contacts without explicit permission
  • We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do NOT use your content or pet data for advertising

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the PawConnect service
  • Display your posts and pet profiles to other users, according to the features you use
  • Deliver messages and notifications you have enabled
  • Enable location-based services (vet finder, dog parks, groomers, walks)
  • Answer your questions to PawConnect Support and send you service announcements
  • Keep the community safe: review reports, apply our Terms, and prevent abuse (see Section 7)
  • Provide the PawAI assistant (see Section 5)
  • Provide pet recovery tags and lost-pet alerts (see Section 6A)
  • Improve the app, measure performance, and fix problems

4. Storage and Security

  • Cloud Storage: Your content, messages, and account records are stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) in Singapore and protected in transit with TLS/SSL encryption. Sign-in, analytics, and push notification services are operated by Google and may process data outside Singapore
  • Photos and Videos: Stored on Cloudflare R2. Profile and pet photos, post photos, stories, Zoomies, and announcement images are public files: the app shows them only to people using it, but anyone who has the exact web address of a file can open it without signing in. Photos, voice notes, and videos in chats, health records, medical documents, and walks are private: they can be opened only through a short-lived link that our servers give to people who are allowed to see them. When you remove a profile or pet photo, or delete a post, the stored file is deleted. Stories leave view after 24 hours; a private copy stays in your Story Archive, and the story's files are kept for 30 days after it ends (longer if you add it to a highlight) and then deleted. A copy that someone saved, or that another app or device has cached, is outside our control
  • Authentication: Passwords are never stored in plain text; Firebase Authentication handles sign-in
  • Messages: Messages are stored on our servers so they can be delivered and kept in your chat history. They are not end-to-end encrypted
  • Walk Data: A draft of a walk is kept on your device until it is saved, you discard it, or you sign out. A finished walk, including its route, is stored on our servers with your pet's activity log (see Section 2.2)

5. PawAI Assistant

PawAI is powered by OpenAI, a third-party AI service. When you ask PawAI a question, we send OpenAI your question and earlier messages in the chat, the details of the pets you pick (such as name, species, breed, age, and weight, and the notes you wrote about them), the care records PawAI needs to answer (such as health records and the notes inside them, activity, and reminders), photos or vet documents you ask about, and the notes PawAI has saved for you if "Remember useful details" is on. We do not send your account details (your name, email address, or phone number) or your pet's microchip number. Everything you type yourself, in a question or in a note, is sent as you wrote it, so please leave out personal details you do not want shared. If PawAI looks something up on the web, OpenAI turns your question into a search and may see your approximate country. We ask OpenAI not to store the request, and we identify you to OpenAI only with an anonymous identifier. OpenAI processes this information to produce the answer; see OpenAI's privacy policy (Section 13).

In the current version of the app, PawAI asks you to agree before you use it for the first time, and you can withdraw your agreement at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security; after you withdraw, the app does not send your questions to PawAI. Older versions of the app do not ask. "Remember useful details" is off until you turn it on. Your PawAI conversation history and saved notes are stored in your account and are deleted when you delete your account or clear them. When you delete your account, your PawAI agreement record is deleted with it.

PawAI is not a veterinarian. Its answers are general information and not a substitute for professional veterinary advice.

6. Messages, Support, and Announcements

  • Messages: Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Anyone in a chat can keep a copy (for example by taking a screenshot or starring a message), which we cannot control. In a one-to-one chat the sender can see when you have read a message. In a group chat (up to 200 people) the app stores how far each member has read, and people in the group can tap "Seen by" on their own message to see who has seen it
  • PawConnect Support: When you use Settings → Chat With Support, your messages and photos are stored in a support conversation that our team can read and reply to. Our team may add private notes about the conversation, which you cannot see. Support may send an automatic acknowledgement, and conversations you leave unanswered may be marked resolved automatically after a period. We limit how fast messages can be sent to Support to prevent abuse
  • Announcements: We may send service announcements and updates to the read-only PawConnect Updates chat in the app, with a push notification if you have message notifications turned on
  • Push Notifications: Delivered through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Your device token is removed when you sign out or delete your account. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings or in the app

6A. Pet Recovery Page (QR Tag) and Lost Mode

If you create a recovery tag for a pet, anyone who scans the QR code or opens its link can see a public page, without signing in. You choose what it shows: the pet's name and species and, if you turn them on, its breed, photo, and microchip number; your name and phone number; an optional second contact; a message to the finder; a medical alert; and, if you turn on Lost Mode, where the pet was last seen. We record that the tag was scanned (the time only) and notify you and the pet's co-owners. A finder can send you a message, and may choose to include their phone number and location; this is stored with the pet and visible to you and the pet's co-owners. You can change what is shown, switch the page off, or issue a new QR code at any time (the old link then stops working). Deleting the pet or your account deletes the tag and the reports sent to it. The page is served from our website host (GitHub Pages) and loads its details from our Google Cloud servers, so those providers receive a finder's IP address, the tag's link, and anything the finder chooses to send, even though the finder has no account with us.

7. Safety: Reports, Blocking, and Moderation

  • Reports: If you report a post, comment, story, or person, we store the report (what was reported, the reason, any details you add, and who filed it). Our team can see who filed a report; the person you reported is not told
  • Blocking: When you block someone, the app hides each other's posts, stories, pets, profile, and comments from both of you, and our servers refuse new messages, follows, paws, reactions, comments, and notifications between you. Any follow between you ends, and any pet access you had shared with each other ends. You may both still be members of group chats you were already in together; in those chats the app hides the other person's messages from you. The hiding of other people's content in lists is done by the app on your phone, so a very old version of the app may keep showing some of it
  • Automatic screening: Text you post in posts, comments, and stories, and the first picture of a post or story, are sent to OpenAI's moderation service when they are created, so that harmful material can be found. Direct messages and profile details are not screened. A serious match is reported to our team and may be hidden until a person has reviewed it; nothing is deleted automatically, and you can ask us to look again through Chat With Support or support@pawconnectsocial.com
  • Moderation actions: Our team may remove content, restrict an account's ability to post, comment, or share stories, or close an account that breaks our Terms. We keep a log of these actions (what was done, to what, by whom, and why, including the username shown at the time) for 12 months. The text of removed content is deleted from the log if its author deletes their account
  • Restricted accounts: A restricted account can still browse, message, and contact PawConnect Support, for example to ask us to review a decision. Whether an account is restricted is private: only that person and our team can see it
  • Reporting abuse of our team: To limit abuse, an account that files an unusually large number of reports in a short time may be temporarily unable to file more

8. Data Sharing

  • Public Posts and Stories: Content you post publicly is visible to all PawConnect users, and its files can be opened by anyone who has their web address (see Section 4). Only you can see who watched, pawed, or reacted to your story. Anyone who can see a post can see who pawed or reacted to it
  • Recovery Page: If you create a pet recovery tag, the information you choose to show is visible to anyone who opens the page (see Section 6A)
  • Service Providers: We use Google (Firebase, Google Sign-In, and Google Maps and Places for searching nearby places), Apple (Sign-In, Push Notifications, Maps, which the app uses instead when Google is not available, App Attest), Cloudflare (media storage and delivery), and OpenAI (the PawAI assistant) to run the service. They process data only to provide those services to us. We also use Klipy (GIF search in chats: the search words you type are sent to Klipy, and GIFs you open in a chat are loaded from Klipy's servers) and Open Pet Food Facts (the barcode you scan is sent to this public database to look up a pet-food product)
  • PawShop and Affiliate Links: When you tap the Buy button (for example "Buy on Shopee"), we record the click against your account and open the retailer with a random click id (it does not contain your name or account id). The retailer tells us, through its affiliate programme, when an order was placed with that id, so we can match it to your click and earn a commission; we do not see what else you bought. In the app we may ask whether you got the product and store your answer and an optional rating, and we store the products you save (the hearts) with your account so we can tell you about price drops. When you search PawShop for something we do not list yet, the live results are kept in a private record for your account, and they are deleted with it. PawShop shows a line saying that PawConnect may earn a commission, under Featured items and next to the Buy button. PawShop also lists products from a Shopee store run by the person who operates PawConnect. Those listings show the seller's name, are not affiliate links, and earn no affiliate commission; when you tap one, we add one to a total for that listing and day (the total is not a record of who tapped) and open the store's page with the plain link, without a click id, and we do not match the tap to your account. We do not share your name, email, or account id with the retailer. PawShop is a shop window only: you cannot buy inside PawConnect. The Buy button opens the retailer's own website, where you place and pay for the order; that website has its own privacy policy, which applies there. We receive no payment details or delivery address from it; the order report we get from the retailer's affiliate programme lists the items and quantities ordered and our commission
  • Legal and Safety: If required by law, or to protect the safety of people, animals, or the service

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Correction: View and edit most of your data within the app. To ask for a copy of your personal data or a correction we cannot make in the app, contact us (Section 14)
  • Delete: Delete your account at any time (Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account). See Section 10 for exactly what is deleted and what is kept
  • Location: Revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings
  • Notifications: Turn off notification types in app Settings or iOS Settings
  • Withdraw Consent and Complaints: You can withdraw consent for optional uses (such as location, notifications, or, in the current version of the app, sending your questions to PawAI's AI provider) at any time. Analytics and crash reporting have no switch in the app (Section 2.2). If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, contact us first; you may also contact the data protection authority where you live, such as Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission

10. Deleting Your Account: What Is Deleted and What Is Kept

When you delete your account, deletion starts straight away and removes your account from our records, together with the data associated with it that we are not required or entitled to keep. The deletion runs on our servers, so it carries on if you close the app or lose your connection, and it is retried until it is complete.

10.1 Deleted

  • Your profile, username, sign-in account, and private settings
  • Your pets and their health records, medical documents, reminders, activity, and shared access. If a pet has a co-owner, the pet goes to the co-owner with its records, photos, and history instead (the Delete Account screen tells you before you confirm)
  • Your posts, stories, highlights, and comments, and the photos and videos attached to them
  • Your paws, reactions, story views, follows, followers, bookmarks, hearts and saved products in PawShop, PawShop clicks and answers, notifications, and blocks
  • Your PawAI conversations and memories, your push notification tokens, and your achievements
  • The content of the messages you sent in chats, including photos and voice notes
  • Other people's saved or shared copies of your messages and of your shared posts and stories, where we hold them
  • Your PawConnect Updates chat, and any private notes our team wrote about your support conversations
  • Reports filed about you

10.2 Kept, and why

  • The other people's side of conversations. Your messages in chats with other people stay in their chat history only as "This message was deleted", under the name "Deleted User", and your photo is removed. Messages other people sent you stay with them, because they are their own messages. A chat is removed entirely once no one in it still has an account
  • Our replies in support conversations. Your own messages are removed; our replies remain on a closed conversation marked as a deleted account
  • Reports you filed about other people. They stay so we can act on them, without your name
  • A limited safety record. If at the time you delete your account you were banned, had been blocked by other people, or had reports filed against you, we keep a small record to stop banned people and abusers from simply creating a new account. It holds only whether you were banned, the IDs of the people who had blocked you, and the number and types of reports, stored under a one-way hash of your sign-in identity (email, Apple, or Google). It contains no name or email address and is used for nothing else. If you sign up again with the same identity while it exists, a ban, blocks, and a review flag can be applied to the new account. We keep it for 12 months, or 24 months if you were banned, and then delete it automatically. Our legitimate interest is keeping the community safe
  • Moderation log entries, as described in Section 7, for 12 months (the excerpt of removed content is deleted straight away)
  • Backups and logs. Residual copies in encrypted backups and system logs are overwritten or expire after a limited period and are not used for any other purpose
  • Legal obligations. Anything we are required by law to keep, or need to keep to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims
  • Things we cannot reach. Mentions of your @username in other people's posts or comments remain as plain text, and anything others copied (such as screenshots) is outside our control. Analytics and crash data held by Google follows Google's own retention settings

11. How Long We Keep Information

  • Account and content data: until you delete it or your account
  • Support conversations: until you or we delete the account (see Section 10)
  • Moderation log: 12 months
  • Recent Sign-ins: 90 days
  • Stories: leave view after 24 hours; the files stay in your Story Archive for 30 days after that (longer for stories in a highlight)
  • Safety record after account deletion: 12 months, or 24 months if you were banned
  • Rate-limit and abuse-prevention counters: only as long as needed to apply the limit

12. Children's Privacy

PawConnect is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

  • OpenAI: Generating PawAI answers, and screening posts, comments, and stories for harmful material — Privacy Policy

14. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date, and in the app where appropriate.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise any of your rights, contact us at:
