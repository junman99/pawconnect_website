PawConnect Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PawConnect ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile photo, and pet profiles you create
  • Content: Photos, videos, captions, and comments you post in the app
  • Pet Data: Pet name, species, breed, age, and health notes you enter

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: Approximate location (when you use map-based features like Find a Vet or Dog Parks) — only when you grant permission
  • Device Information: Device type, iOS version, app version
  • Usage Data: Features used and time spent (only if analytics opted-in)
  • Walk Tracking: GPS route data during walks — stored locally and only uploaded if you choose to share

2.3 Information NOT Collected

  • We do NOT access your contacts without explicit permission
  • We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do NOT use your content or pet data for advertising

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the PawConnect service
  • Display your posts and pet profiles to other users
  • Enable location-based services (vet finder, dog parks, groomers)
  • Send notifications you have enabled (likes, comments, new followers)
  • Improve the app and develop new features

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Cloud Storage: Your data is stored securely on Firebase (Google Cloud) servers with TLS/SSL encryption
  • Images: Photos and videos are stored on Cloudflare R2 with private access controls
  • Authentication: Passwords are never stored in plain text; Firebase Authentication handles all auth securely
  • Walk Data: GPS routes are stored locally on your device by default

5. Data Sharing

  • Public Posts: Content you post publicly is visible to all PawConnect users
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and storage, and Cloudflare for media delivery
  • Affiliate Links: When you tap "Buy Now" in PawShop, we record the click (product name, timestamp) to track affiliate commissions. No personal data is shared with the retailer beyond the standard referral link
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data at any time (Settings → Account → Delete Account)
  • Location: Revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings
  • Notifications: Disable any notification type in app Settings

7. Children's Privacy

PawConnect is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and cloud storage — Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In: Authentication — Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare R2: Media storage and delivery — Privacy Policy
  • Apple Maps / MapKit: Location services for vet and service finder — Privacy Policy

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
