Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Your page, your menu, photos, offers and your numbers are free. We don't take a commission on what pet owners pay you.

Do you take bookings or payments?

No. Pet owners message or call you and you book them as you do today. PawConnect brings them to you with what they want already written.

Can pet owners leave reviews?

Not star reviews. They can say whether they would go back and what they paid, anonymously, and post photos of their visit. You see the same numbers.

Can my staff help run the page?

Yes. Invite them as managers from the app. They can edit the page and see the numbers; only you manage the team.

Why sign in with my phone?

There is no password to forget or steal: only a phone signed in to PawConnect can let a computer in, and you can sign it out from the app.
