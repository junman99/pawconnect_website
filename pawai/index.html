How do I switch Mochi to a new food?

Switch Mochi’s food gradually over about a week, and watch how she responds.

Mix the new food into her current food, increasing the new food little by little over 7–10 days. If she develops vomiting, diarrhea, refuses food, or seems unwell, pause the transition and contact your vet. For a prescription diet or a health condition, check with your vet before switching.

Gradual transition
  1. Days 1–2 About 25% new food and 75% current food.
  2. Days 3–4 About half new food and half current food.
  3. Days 5–6 About 75% new food and 25% current food.
  4. Days 7–10 Feed the new food alone if Mochi is tolerating the change.

My dog Biscuit just ate a handful of grapes!

Grapes can be toxic to dogs—contact a veterinarian immediately for Biscuit.

Call a local veterinarian or emergency veterinary clinic now and tell them Biscuit ate a handful of grapes; don’t wait for symptoms. Do not induce vomiting or try home treatment unless a vet instructs you.

Act nowContact a veterinarian immediately. Tell them when Biscuit ate the grapes and roughly how many; keep any packaging or remaining grapes handy.

What PawAI does not do

  • PawAI is not a vet. It gives general information.
  • PawAI does not diagnose your pet and does not give medicine doses. For anything that worries you, contact your vet.
  • PawAI can make mistakes. Check what it tells you.
  • PawAI does not save anything by itself: a reminder or record needs your tap on Confirm.
  • PawAI does not call a food suitable because a maker or a shop says so. If it cannot find reliable information, it says so.
  • PawAI has a daily allowance. The app shows how many answers you have left.

How PawAI uses your data

  • In the current version of the app, PawAI asks you to agree before first use. You can stop at any time and delete what it saved.
  • To write an answer, PawAI sends your question, the pictures you attach and the records of the pets you choose (up to three) to OpenAI, a third-party AI provider.
  • PawAI only remembers details if you turn memory on, and you confirm health facts.

Read the Privacy Policy. Questions or a problem with an answer? Use the Support page, or "Report a problem" under any answer in the app.
