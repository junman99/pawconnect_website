Ask about your
pet's records.

PawAI looks at the pets you choose and answers everyday questions about food, weight and routines, with a chart when it helps.

PawAI can make mistakes and it is not a vet: if something worries you, call your vet. You agree before you first use it, and you can withdraw in Settings.

  • Beta
  • Powered by OpenAI
  • You choose which pets it sees

In the picture: the question “Is Mochi's weight healthy?”, an answer that the weight looks steady, and a weight chart.

If a pet
goes missing.

A QR tag on the collar opens a page that shows only what you chose to show. A finder can call, text or message you without installing anything. Turn on Lost Mode and the page says your pet is missing.

  • QR tag
  • Pet ID card
  • Lost Mode

In the picture: a Pet ID card for a cat, with a phone number to call, the owner's name and a QR code, and a button to share or print the card.
