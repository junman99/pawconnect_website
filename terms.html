7. PawAI Assistant

PawAI is an AI assistant that answers questions about your pets. It is powered by a third-party AI service (OpenAI). Its answers are produced automatically, may be incomplete or wrong, and are general information only: PawAI is not a veterinarian and does not give veterinary advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Do not rely on it in an emergency or instead of seeing a vet; if your pet may be unwell, contact a vet. Check anything PawAI suggests, including a record or reminder it proposes to add, before you act on it. What you type and the pet details you choose are sent to the AI service as described in the Privacy Policy, so do not enter details you do not want shared. We may limit how often PawAI can be used.

8. Recovery Tags and Lost Mode

A recovery tag gives a pet a QR code and a public web page. You choose what the page shows, and anyone who opens the link can see it without signing in, so show only what you are comfortable making public. You can switch the page off or issue a new QR code at any time; the old link then stops working. We do not promise that a tag, Lost Mode, or an alert will help a pet to be found, that a finder will scan or act on a tag, or that a notification will reach you. Finders can send you messages, phone numbers and locations; PawConnect does not check what finders send. Keep your pet's microchip registration with the microchip registry up to date: a PawConnect tag does not replace it.

9. Sharing a Pet with Other People

You can share a pet with co-owners, caregivers and viewers by invite or by a join code. What each person can see and do depends on the role you give them: for example, every role can see the pet's activity, including walk routes, and co-owners can also see the pet's private details and change its information. You are responsible for choosing who you share with and can remove anyone's access at any time; blocking a person also ends access you shared with each other. Only invite people who agree to receive access, and only enter details of other people (such as emergency contacts) if they are happy for you to do so. If you delete your account and a pet has a co-owner, the pet is handed to the co-owner with its records.

10. Health Information and Notifications

Health records, vaccination dates, medication, weight, reminders, and feeding or calorie guides in PawConnect are tools to help you keep records; they are not veterinary advice, and calorie targets are estimates. Reminders and notifications depend on your phone's settings, battery, connection and permissions, and on our service, and may arrive late or not at all: do not rely on them as your only way of remembering medicine, vaccines or appointments. Insurance paperwork you upload is stored like any other document; PawConnect does not sell, compare or arrange pet insurance.
